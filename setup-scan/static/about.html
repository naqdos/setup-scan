About

I got interested in the markets when I was 14. I started studying every day — reading, watching charts, testing ideas — and slowly building up a set of strategies I actually trusted. It took about two years before I was consistently profitable. Along the way I noticed that the tools I was using were scattered: technicals in one place, fundamentals in another, backtesting somewhere else entirely, and no single place that pulled it all together. I don't think there's a perfect indicator, but I wanted a way to organize both technical and fundamental signals in one place instead of jumping between a dozen tabs. Setup Scan is my attempt at that.

What this is

Setup Scan combines technical screening (momentum, relative strength, volume), a custom scoring model, historical backtesting of specific trade setups, and company fundamentals — into a single tool instead of several disconnected ones.

Built with

  • Python + Flask backend
  • Live market data via yfinance
  • A custom momentum scoring model (see Methodology)
  • A backtesting engine for three trade setups
  • Fundamentals (valuation, growth, profitability, analyst view)

A note on accuracy

Backtested results describe how a setup performed historically on specific tickers — they are not a guarantee of future performance. The momentum score is one way of organizing signals, not a claim that it's the "correct" way to value a stock.
